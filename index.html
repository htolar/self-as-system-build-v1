```html Interactive Blobs
i

System Name: The Band

Rules

1. Split — when clicking space the current blobs on screen split into two.

2. Draggability — when clicking and dragging with the cursor on the blob it moves around.

3. Deformation — when the cursor hovers over the blob it deforms to where the cursor is almost like water.

4. Color and glow — when you hover over any of the blobs with your cursor it turns a random color then fades.

5. Interaction — when the blobs go next to/on top of each other they get repelled/they push off each other.

Controls

Press space to split blobs in half.

Hover over the blobs to both deform them and make them light up a random colour.

To get the full effect press space a good amount until you see many blobs then hover over them.

Reflection

This newest version of my simulation, I feel this represents me in the band I play in. I'm sure I can create cool things by myself but it's more fantastic with others.

This system is different from my other tests in which it has the rule split and the rule glow, and it's a lot more strict with the rules now and the rules overall look better.

However it starts to feel way too noisy when the blob content gets too high in which the browser starts to lag. In V2 I will add more of a cap to the amount of blobs a high cap but not high enough to lag the system.

Click Space to Start
```
